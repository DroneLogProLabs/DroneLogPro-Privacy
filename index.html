DroneLogPro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026
Last updated: October 2, 2026

DroneLogPro is a drone operations and recordkeeping application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information You Enter

DroneLogPro allows users to enter and manage information such as flight records, aircraft details, battery records, maintenance information, pilot information, reports, and notes. This information is stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export, share, back up, or restore it.

DroneLogProLabs does not operate a remote account system or independently collect these locally stored records.

2. Advertising and Automatically Collected Information

The free version of DroneLogPro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, general location information, diagnostics, and app usage information. The exact information processed may depend on the user's device, region, privacy selections, and advertising settings.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services .

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

DroneLogPro uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent or present privacy choices where required. Depending on the user's location, users may be able to consent to personalized advertising, select non-personalized advertising, object to certain processing, or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

When required, privacy choices can be reviewed or changed through the privacy options available in the app.

4. Data Sharing

DroneLogProLabs does not sell the flight, aircraft, battery, maintenance, pilot, report, or note information that users enter into DroneLogPro. Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in their privacy policies and according to the user's consent selections.

User-created records are only exported or shared when the user chooses to use an export, backup, restore, or sharing feature.

5. Data Storage and Security

User-created DroneLogPro records are primarily stored on the user's device. Users are responsible for protecting their device and any files they export or back up. Reasonable technical measures are used within the app, but no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored records remain on the device until the user edits or deletes them, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. Exported or backed-up files remain wherever the user saved them until the user deletes them.

7. Children's Privacy

DroneLogPro is intended for drone pilots, operators, and aviation professionals. It is not directed toward children under 13, and DroneLogProLabs does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

8. User Rights

Depending on their location, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or consent withdrawal. Locally stored information can generally be managed directly inside the app or removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when DroneLogPro's features, services, or legal obligations change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact DroneLogProLabs through the public support page: DroneLogPro Support .
